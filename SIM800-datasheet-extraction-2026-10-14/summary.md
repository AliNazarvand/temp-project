## Interpretation notes

- Family-level AT command support is recorded in `shared_at_command_catalog.json`; it is not a claim of support by every model.
- Each per-model JSON contains pinout, reference circuits, PCB/antenna/protection guidance, validation details, missing fields, ambiguities, and page citations.
- Empty or null values mean the supplied source did not establish the requested fact; they do not mean the capability is absent.
